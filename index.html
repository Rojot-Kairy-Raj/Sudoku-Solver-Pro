About Sudoku Solver Pro

What is Sudoku?

Sudoku is a number puzzle played on a 9×9 grid. The goal is to fill every empty cell with numbers from 1 to 9.

How to Play

• Each row must contain 1–9 without repetition.
• Each column must contain 1–9 without repetition.
• Each 3×3 box must contain 1–9 without repetition.
• Enter numbers in empty cells to solve the puzzle.

Game Features

• Easy, Medium & Hard difficulty
• 3 Lives
• Limited Hints
• Timer
• Remaining Number Progress
• Rating after completing a puzzle
• Sound Effects

Tip

Use logic and check the row, column and 3×3 box before entering a number.
